**Summary:** Early blight of potato is caused by *Alternaria solani* and appears as brown‑black angular spots with concentric rings on leaves; it can spread quickly and reduce yield if not controlled. [1]

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Use disease‑free seed tubers for planting. [1]  
- Remove and destroy all infected plant debris after harvest. [1]  
- Plant the resistant variety Kufri Sindhuri if available. [1]  
- Maintain good field hygiene and avoid excessive nitrogen on poorly manured fields. [1]

**Chemical options:**  
- Spray Zineb 0.2 % early in the season and repeat every 15–20 days. [1][2]  
- Spray Captan 0.2 % early in the season and repeat every 15–20 days. [1][2]

**When to get expert help:**  
- If disease appears despite early sprays and cultural measures. [1]  
- If you cannot obtain disease‑free seed or resistant varieties. [1]  
- If you need assistance selecting or applying fungicides safely. [1]

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
