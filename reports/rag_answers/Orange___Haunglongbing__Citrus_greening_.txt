Orange___Haunglongbing_(Citrus_greening)
mode: llm

**Summary:** Huanglongbing (citrus greening) is a bacterial disease that stunts growth, causes leaf mottling and produces small, misshapen, low‑quality fruit; it can eventually kill the tree if not managed[1][2].  

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Use only pathogen‑free budwood for all new plantings[1].  
- Maintain good overall tree health, especially root health, to help trees tolerate the disease[4].  
- Monitor and control the Asian citrus psyllid (Diaphorina citri) with insecticides as needed[1].  

**Chemical options:**  
- Apply tetracycline at 500 ppm as a spray, repeating every two weeks[1].  

**When to get expert help:**  
- If you suspect infection, have leaf or psyllid samples tested by a diagnostic lab (PCR)[4].  
- If PCR confirms HLB, follow mandatory insecticide treatment and consider removal of the infected tree[4].  
- Seek advice on sample collection protocols from the local citrus pest detection program[4].

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
