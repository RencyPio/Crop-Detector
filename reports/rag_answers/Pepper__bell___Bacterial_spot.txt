Pepper,_bell___Bacterial_spot
mode: llm

**Summary:** Bacterial spot on bell pepper is caused by *Xanthomonas campestris* and can cause leaf loss, stem cankers and fruit lesions, reducing yield significantly. [1]

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Use disease‑free seed and practice field sanitation. [1]  
- Avoid spraying streptomycin after fruits have started to form. [1]  

**Chemical options:**  
- Spray mancozeb at 2 g per litre of water. [1]  
- Spray copper oxychloride at 2.5 g per litre of water. [1]  
- Treat seed with 0.1 % mercuric chloride solution for 2–5 minutes. [1]  
- Spray seedlings with Bordeaux mixture at 1 % or copper oxychloride at 0.25 %. [1]  

**When to get expert help:**  
- If disease spreads rapidly despite these measures. [1]  
- If you need assistance selecting certified disease‑free seed. [1]

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
