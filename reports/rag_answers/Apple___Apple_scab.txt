Apple___Apple_scab
mode: llm

**Summary:** Apple scab is caused by *Venturia inaequalis* and can severely distort leaves and fruit, reducing yield. It is serious if not managed early. [1]

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Remove and destroy fallen leaves and pruned material during winter to break the disease cycle. [1]  
- Apply 5 % urea before leaf fall in autumn to hasten leaf decomposition. [1]  
- Apply 2 % urea before bud break to further speed leaf breakdown. [1]  

**Chemical options:**  
- Spray Tridemorph 0.1 % before flowering. [1]  
- Spray Mancozeb 0.25 % at the bearing stage. [1]  
- At pink bud (≈15 days after first spray): apply Captan 250 g or Mancozeb 300 g per 100 L water. [1]  
- At petal fall: apply Carbendazim 50 g per 100 L water. [1]  
- Ten days after petal fall: apply Captan 200 g or Mancozeb 300 g per 100 L water. [1]  
- Fourteen days after fruit set: apply Captafol 150 g per 100 L water, adding stickers (teepol or triton) 6 ml per 10 L spray fluid. [1]  

**When to get expert help:**  
- If disease symptoms persist despite the above sprays.  
- If you cannot obtain the listed fungicides locally.  
- If weather conditions (heavy rain, high humidity) favor rapid disease spread and you need timing advice.

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
