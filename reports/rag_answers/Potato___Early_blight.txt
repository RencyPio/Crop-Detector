Potato___Early_blight
mode: llm

**Summary:** Early blight of potato is caused by *Alternaria solani* and can severely damage leaves, tubers and yield if not controlled early [1].

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Plant disease‑free seed tubers [1].  
- Remove and destroy infected plant debris to reduce soil inoculum [1].  
- Use the moderately resistant variety Kufri Sindhuri if available [1][2].

**Chemical options:**  
- Spray Zineb 0.2 % very early and repeat every 15–20 days for effective control [1][2].  
- Spray Captan 0.2 % very early and repeat every 15–20 days for effective control [1][2].

**When to get expert help:**  
- Not found in the knowledge base.

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
