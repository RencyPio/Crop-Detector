**What the agricultural guides say about Cherry (including sour) - Powdery mildew:**

[1] On This Page Comments on the Disease Powdery mildew is marked by superficial, white, weblike growth on leaves, shoots, or fruit. Comments on the Disease The fungus overwinters in buds on twigs and as chasmothecia, which are spore-containing structures, on the bark of twigs and branches. Secondary spores produced in spring spread the fungus to new growth. Infections can be severe in commercial orchards during years of low rainfall, high humidity, and warm temperatures (70° to 80°F).
