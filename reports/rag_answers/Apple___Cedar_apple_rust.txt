**What the agricultural guides say about Apple - Cedar apple rust:**

[1] Rusts of Apple Date: Michael A. Ellis, Department of Plant Pathology Three rust diseases occur on apple and crabapple in Ohio. All are caused by different species of the fungus Gymnosporangium and have various junipers and red cedars (juniperus species) as an alternate host. Apples are generally most susceptible to infection by the rust fungi during the period from early bloom until about 30 days after bloom.
