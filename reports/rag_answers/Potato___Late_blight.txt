Potato___Late_blight
mode: llm

**Summary:** Potato late blight is a serious disease caused by *Phytophthora infestans* that attacks leaves, stems and tubers, leading to rapid crop loss if not managed promptly. [2]

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Plant resistant varieties such as Kufri Naveen, Kufri Jeevan, Kufri Alenkar, Kufri Khasi Garo or Kufri Moti. [2]  
- Remove and destroy infected foliage a few days before harvest; spray a suitable herbicide to aid foliage destruction. [2]  
- Avoid injuries to tubers at harvest and do not store visibly infected tubers. [2]  
- Keep fields dry and reduce leaf wetness by improving field drainage and avoiding overhead irrigation during humid periods. [2]

**Chemical options:**  
- Spray mancozeb at 0.2 % concentration as a protective spray to prevent tuber infection. [2]  
- Spray zineb at 0.2 % concentration as a protective spray to prevent tuber infection. [2]

**When to get expert help:**  
- If disease appears despite protective sprays and cultural measures. [2]  
- When tuber loss exceeds 10 % of the expected yield. [2]  
- If you need assistance selecting appropriate herbicides for foliage destruction before harvest. [2]

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
