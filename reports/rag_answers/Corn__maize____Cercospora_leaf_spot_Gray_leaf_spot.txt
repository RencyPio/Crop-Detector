**What the agricultural guides say about Corn (maize) - Cercospora leaf spot Gray leaf spot:**

[1] Gray Leaf Spot of Corn Gray leaf spot, caused by the fungus Cercospora zeae-maydis, occurs virtually every growing season. If conditions favor disease development, economic losses can occur. Symptoms first appear on lower leaves about two to three weeks before tasseling. The leaf lesions are long (up to 2 inches), narrow, rectangular, and light tan colored. Later, the lesions can turn gray. They are usually delimited by leaf veins but can join together and kill entire leaves. Rectangular gray leaf spot lesions delimited by leaf veins. Image: A.
