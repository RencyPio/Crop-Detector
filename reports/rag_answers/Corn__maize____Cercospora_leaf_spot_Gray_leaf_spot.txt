Corn_(maize)___Cercospora_leaf_spot Gray_leaf_spot
mode: llm

**Summary:** Gray leaf spot of corn, caused by *Cercospora* fungus, can cause serious yield loss if weather is warm and humid. It appears on lower leaves before tasseling and can kill whole leaves. [1]

**What to do now (cultural / non‑chemical):**  
- Plant resistant hybrids or inbreds when possible. [1]  
- Rotate corn with non‑cereal crops to break the disease cycle. [1]  
- Practice tillage to destroy corn residue that harbors the fungus. [1]  

**Chemical options:**  
- Not found in the knowledge base.  

**When to get expert help:**  
- Disease spreads rapidly despite cultural measures.  
- You need a fungicide but have no label information.  
- Symptoms are severe and affecting yield.

Always follow the pesticide label and consult your local agriculture officer before applying any chemical.
